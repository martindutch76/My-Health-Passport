Linking Key Table Generator

This tool converts an existing set of SLK-581 statistical linkage keys into My Health Passport Barcodes, generated as one-way SHA-256 hashes.

The output is a linkage table: each row pairs an original SLK-581 with its corresponding My Health Passport Barcode, allowing the two to be linked against your current SLK-581-enabled health datasets.

All data is transformed locally within your dashboard, in your browser. No data is transferred to our servers, or recorded in the cloud.

This converter is designed for small, Excel-spreadsheet-sized projects — everything is held in your browser's working memory rather than processed in chunks on a server. Recommended for batches of less than 250,000 rows. Larger files may slow down or strain the browser, especially on older or memory-constrained devices; for anything approaching that scale, consider using our SQL-based coding approaches instead.
