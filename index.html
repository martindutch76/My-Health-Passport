My Health Passport Number Technology

Your My Health Passport Number is generated using the SLK-581 (Statistical Linkage Key 581) — a privacy-preserving data linkage key used across Australia's health data environment.

What is SLK-581?

SLK-581 is a statistical linkage key format developed by the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare (AIHW). It's designed to bring records relating to the same person together across different datasets — without revealing their identity.

How is it generated?

Your identifying information is used on your device to create a unique SLK-581 key. The key is built from selected letters and digits of your:

First name Surname Date of birth Sex

These elements are converted into a coded, one-way key. Reverse engineering the key to successfully identify you by name is very unlikely.

Its purpose and use

SLK-581 allows data from different sources to be linked for research and planning purposes, while protecting personal privacy. When appropriately approved, it lets a researcher link data from their study with other health datasets, without knowing, sharing or recording personal identifying information like your name, date of birth, address or sex.

Widely used in Australian health data

SLK-581 is one of the most widely used linkage-key formats in Australian health and community-service data collections. It was developed by the AIHW and has been applied in hospital, aged-care and national registry data linkage for over a decade.

This tool is an independent, open implementation of the SLK-581 format — it isn't an official AIHW product.

Privacy and Performance

My Health Passport is designed with privacy at its core, while using a linkage approach that has been evaluated in Australian health-data research.

Your information stays with you

The identifying information you enter to create your My Health Passport Number is processed locally within your browser. This page does not send that information to My Health Passport servers for processing.

The implementation is designed not to place identifying inputs in cookies, local storage or session storage. The generated Passport Number is what you can copy and choose to share.

Almost completely unique

Your Passport Number is derived from the core demographic elements used by SLK-581. The combination is designed to make accidental duplication uncommon, although no statistical linkage key is guaranteed to be unique for every person.

Performance you can trust

Published Australian research has found that SLK-based linkage can achieve very high match quality. In one AIHW evaluation, a stepwise strategy using SLK-581 with additional common variables achieved a 99.7% positive predictive value and 98.5% sensitivity compared with a reference name-based strategy.
